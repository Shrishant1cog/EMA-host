Google API Services User Data Policy Compliance

EMA Smart Universal Email Assistant's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy , including the Limited Use requirements.

1. Information We Access and Collect

When you connect your Google Account to EMA, we request authorization for specific scopes to perform automated calendar scheduling:

  • Profile & Email: To authenticate your account and display connected inboxes.
  • Gmail Data (gmail.modify): We read unread incoming messages and relevant attachments solely to detect calendar invites, appointments, and meeting details. We mark processed messages as read to prevent duplicate scheduling.
  • Google Calendar (calendar): To create, update, and manage your scheduled events.
  • Google Drive (drive.file, drive.appdata): To store application settings and save attachment evidence associated with scheduled events directly inside your own Google Drive.

2. How Your Data Is Processed

Email snippets and extracted event text are evaluated using automated AI parsing (via Groq Cloud inference API) exclusively to structure date, time, and attendee parameters. Data is processed transiently in memory. We do not use your email data to train any generalized AI models.

3. Data Sharing & Third Parties

EMA does not sell, lease, or monetize your personal data. We do not share your Google user data with external advertisers or data brokers under any circumstances. Data transfers only occur with essential cloud infrastructure services strictly needed to operate the core assistant features.

4. Data Storage and Retention

Your authorization credentials and settings are isolated to your account. You can revoke access at any time via the application settings or directly through your Google Security Account Permissions . Revoking access halts all email monitoring and removes authorization tokens.
