Google API Services User Data Policy

EMA's use and transfer of information received from Google APIs complies with the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.

1. Information EMA accesses

When you authorize a Google account, EMA uses the permissions shown during Google's consent flow to provide the features you enable. Depending on those permissions, this can include:

  • Profile and email address: to identify the connected account and show it in the application.
  • Gmail messages and attachments: to inspect relevant unread messages, identify dates and meeting details, and prevent repeated processing. EMA may change a message's read status as part of processing.
  • Google Calendar: to find matching events and create or update events when the extracted details warrant an action.
  • Google Drive: to store application state or settings and, where enabled, save files associated with a processed message.

2. How email content is processed

EMA extracts relevant message text and supported attachment content to identify dates, times, attendees, and event details. The application may send text needed for extraction to its configured model-inference provider, Groq Cloud. The provider processes requests under its own service terms and privacy practices. Do not connect an account unless you are comfortable with this processing.

EMA is not designed to use your email content to train a general-purpose model. Automated extraction can be incomplete or incorrect, so review calendar events before relying on them.

3. Service providers and sharing

EMA does not sell personal data. Information is processed by Google services needed for account access, email, calendar, and Drive functionality; the configured inference provider when extraction is requested; and the hosting providers used to deliver the website and backend. These providers process information under their applicable terms. EMA does not intentionally share Google user data with advertisers or data brokers.

4. Storage and retention

EMA may store account settings, authorization state, processing history, and operational logs needed to run and troubleshoot the service. Some application state may be stored in the connected Google Drive account, while other operational state may be held by EMA's backend. Retention can depend on the feature and storage location. This policy does not promise immediate automatic deletion of every copy.

You can revoke EMA's Google access through Google Account permissions. Revoking access prevents future authorized access once the revocation takes effect, but it does not automatically delete events already created in Calendar or files already saved to Drive.

5. Security and your choices

EMA uses application controls intended to restrict account access and protect session data. No online service can guarantee absolute security. Keep your Google account secure, review the requested permissions, and disconnect the integration if you no longer want EMA to process an account.

6. Privacy requests and contact

For questions, access requests, or deletion requests, contact shrishanthsandigawad1@gmail.com. Please identify the account concerned and the request you are making. We may need to verify account ownership before responding.
